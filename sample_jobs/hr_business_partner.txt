HR Business Partner, Operations (sample job)

About the role
We are hiring an HR Business Partner to support a 500-person operations business. You will be the first point of contact for business heads on everything people-related.

What you'll do
- Partner with business heads on workforce planning, team structures and talent reviews
- Handle employee relations cases fairly and in line with policy
- Run the annual and mid-year performance management cycles for your business unit
- Use HR data (attrition, engagement, hiring) to spot problems early and recommend fixes
- Design and run onboarding and engagement initiatives

What we're looking for
- 4+ years of experience as an HRBP or HR generalist
- Strong employee relations and performance management experience
- Comfortable with HR analytics and Excel
- Able to influence senior stakeholders

Nice to have
- Experience with Workday or another HRIS
- Experience reducing attrition through onboarding or engagement programmes
