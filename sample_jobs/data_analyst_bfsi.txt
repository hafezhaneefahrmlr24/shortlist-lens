Data Analyst, Lending (sample job)

About the role
Our lending team needs an analyst to build the dashboards and analysis behind credit and customer decisions.

What you'll do
- Write SQL to pull and clean loan and customer data
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau or Power BI
- Automate recurring reports in Python
- Support credit-risk analysis with clear findings for business teams

What we're looking for
- 3+ years of experience in data analysis
- Strong SQL and Python
- Experience building dashboards in Tableau or Power BI
- Background in banking, financial services or insurance (BFSI)

Nice to have
- Credit-risk or lending experience
- A good grounding in statistics
